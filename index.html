Export composer
Export is rendered on a separate off-screen map, so its framing and resolution are independent of your browser window.
Map exports use a dedicated off-screen Leaflet map at the selected pixel size and geographic bounds. Larger output sizes make Leaflet request more detailed tiles instead of enlarging a screenshot.
Population and land area: 2020 Census (P.L. 94-171 population, TIGER land area) for every California tract. Tract geometry: Census cartographic boundary files (1:500k) statewide, with street-accurate TIGER/Line geometry for the Los Angeles and Bay Area preset counties. Current Metro rail and stations load from public ArcGIS feature services; future corridors are planning-level schematic alignments and are drawn dashed. The shipped region set is never overwritten; edits live in your browser or in exported JSON.
