Export images
Images are drawn on a separate off-screen map, so framing and resolution don't depend on your window. Neighborhood sheet exports the neighborhood selected in the list, with its comparison table.
Residents and homes: 2020 Census blocks (P.L. 94-171 / DHC), spread over each block's residential lots, so single-lot values are estimates. The Census also adds privacy noise to block counts, so trust groups of blocks more than one block. Lots: Santa Clara County parcel layer. Land is gross acres, streets included. Neighborhood edits live in your browser or in exported JSON; tax data stays in this browser.
